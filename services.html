Consulting

When biology becomes data,
the hard part is knowing what it means

I work with organizations building, studying, or communicating products and ideas around human biology and health — helping them examine what they're measuring, what the evidence supports, and what conclusions their data can responsibly carry.

What are you actually measuring?

Measurement & evidence

Biomarkers, biological measurements, and omics data are only as useful as the validity and quality behind them. I help teams establish whether a measurement is built to answer the question being asked, and how strong the evidence behind it really is.

Includes
Measurement and analytical strategy
Omics and biomarker workflow validation and quality
Evidence-strength assessment

What does it actually mean?

Interpretation & insight

A valid measurement still has to be read correctly. I help teams connect data back to the underlying biology, separate signal from noise, evaluate what a claim actually supports, and understand where a result does or doesn't generalize.

Includes
Biological interpretation of omics and biomarker data
Multi-omics data integration
Claim evaluation and limitations review
What can you build or claim from it?

Strategy & translation

Turning validated evidence into a scientific or product strategy, and into narratives that hold up with the people who matter — reviewers, collaborators, funders, investors, and decision-makers.

Includes
Scientific and biomarker strategy
Evidence plans and scientific narratives for grants and proposals
Investor, partner, and technical due-diligence materials
Need this thinking inside the team?
